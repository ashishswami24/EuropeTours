Itinerary

  • Day 1: Arrive in Paris, explore the Eiffel Tower and Champs-Élysées.

    Begin your French escapade in the romantic city of Paris. After checking into your hotel, visit iconic landmarks such as the Eiffel Tower and Champs-Élysées.

  • Day 2: Paris city tour and Louvre Museum visit.

    Enjoy a guided city tour of Paris, followed by a visit to the world-renowned Louvre Museum. Explore the city's rich cultural heritage and artistic treasures.

  • Day 3: Day trip to Versailles Palace.

    Take a day trip to the magnificent Versailles Palace. Discover the opulent palace, beautiful gardens, and stunning architecture.

  • Day 4: Travel to Lyon, explore Old Lyon and Basilica of Notre-Dame de Fourvière.

    Travel to Lyon and explore the charming Old Lyon district and the Basilica of Notre-Dame de Fourvière. Enjoy the city's culinary delights and historic sites.

  • Day 5: Lyon city tour and visit to Parc de la Tête d'Or.

    Join a guided city tour of Lyon, followed by a visit to the beautiful Parc de la Tête d'Or. Discover the city's rich history and vibrant culture.

  • Day 6: Travel to Nice, relax on the French Riviera.

    Head to Nice and relax on the stunning French Riviera. Enjoy the beautiful beaches, crystal-clear waters, and vibrant atmosphere.

  • Day 7: Nice city tour and visit to Promenade des Anglais.

    Take a guided city tour of Nice and visit the famous Promenade des Anglais. Explore the city's charming streets and beautiful coastline.

  • Day 8: Day trip to Monaco.

    Take a day trip to the glamorous principality of Monaco. Visit the luxurious Monte Carlo Casino, Prince's Palace, and enjoy the stunning views.

  • Day 9: Return to Paris, day at leisure.

    Return to Paris and spend the day at leisure. Explore any remaining attractions, indulge in French cuisine, or simply relax.

  • Day 10: Depart from Paris.

    It's time to say au revoir to France. Depart from Paris with unforgettable memories of your French adventure.
