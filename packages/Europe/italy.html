Itinerary

  • Day 1: Arrive in Rome, explore the Colosseum and Roman Forum.

    Begin your Italian adventure in the historic city of Rome. After checking into your hotel, visit the iconic Colosseum and the ancient Roman Forum.

  • Day 2: Rome city tour and visit to Vatican City.

    Enjoy a guided city tour of Rome and explore the wonders of Vatican City. Visit St. Peter's Basilica, the Vatican Museums, and the Sistine Chapel.

  • Day 3: Day trip to Pompeii.

    Take a day trip to the ancient city of Pompeii, preserved by the eruption of Mount Vesuvius. Discover the archaeological wonders and historical significance of this UNESCO World Heritage site.

  • Day 4: Travel to Florence, visit the Uffizi Gallery and Ponte Vecchio.

    Travel to the charming city of Florence. Explore the Uffizi Gallery, home to masterpieces by Botticelli and Michelangelo, and visit the historic Ponte Vecchio bridge.

  • Day 5: Florence city tour and visit to the Duomo.

    Join a guided city tour of Florence and visit the magnificent Florence Cathedral, also known as the Duomo. Discover the city's rich artistic heritage and Renaissance architecture.

  • Day 6: Day trip to Pisa and Lucca.

    Take a day trip to the cities of Pisa and Lucca. Visit the famous Leaning Tower of Pisa and explore the medieval charm of Lucca.

  • Day 7: Travel to Venice, explore St. Mark's Square and the Grand Canal.

    Head to the romantic city of Venice. Visit St. Mark's Square, St. Mark's Basilica, and take a leisurely gondola ride along the enchanting Grand Canal.

  • Day 8: Venice island tour: Murano, Burano, and Torcello.

    Embark on an island-hopping tour of Venice, visiting Murano, famous for its glassmaking, Burano, known for its colorful houses and lace, and the historic island of Torcello.

  • Day 9: Day at leisure in Venice.

    Spend a relaxing day at leisure in Venice. Explore the city's hidden gems, enjoy Venetian cuisine, or simply soak in the unique atmosphere.

  • Day 10: Depart from Venice.

    It's time to say arrivederci to Italy. Depart from Venice with unforgettable memories of your Italian journey.
