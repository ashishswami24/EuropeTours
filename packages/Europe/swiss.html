Itinerary

  • Day 1: Arrive in Zurich, explore the city and Bahnhofstrasse.

    Begin your Swiss adventure in Zurich. Check into your hotel and explore the city, including the famous shopping street Bahnhofstrasse.

  • Day 2: Zurich city tour and Lake Zurich cruise.

    Enjoy a guided city tour of Zurich, followed by a relaxing cruise on Lake Zurich. Discover the city's historic landmarks and beautiful scenery.

  • Day 3: Travel to Lucerne, visit Chapel Bridge and Old Town.

    Travel to Lucerne and explore the picturesque Chapel Bridge and charming Old Town. Enjoy the stunning lake and mountain views.

  • Day 4: Day trip to Mount Pilatus.

    Take a day trip to Mount Pilatus and enjoy the breathtaking alpine scenery. Experience activities like hiking, cable car rides, or simply soaking in the views.

  • Day 5: Travel to Interlaken, enjoy adventure activities.

    Head to Interlaken, a hub for adventure sports. Enjoy activities like paragliding, hiking, or simply exploring the beautiful surroundings.

  • Day 6: Day trip to Jungfraujoch - Top of Europe.

    Take an unforgettable day trip to Jungfraujoch, known as the Top of Europe. Experience breathtaking views and the thrill of being at one of the highest points in Europe.

  • Day 7: Explore Interlaken and surroundings.

    Spend the day exploring Interlaken and its surroundings. Visit nearby lakes, take scenic hikes, or relax in the beautiful natural environment.

  • Day 8: Return to Zurich, day at leisure.

    Return to Zurich and spend the day at leisure. Explore any remaining attractions, enjoy Swiss cuisine, or simply relax.

  • Day 9: Depart from Zurich.

    It's time to say goodbye to Switzerland. Depart from Zurich with unforgettable memories of your Swiss adventure.
