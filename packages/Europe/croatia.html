Itinerary

  • Day 1: Arrive in Dubrovnik, explore the Old Town and City Walls.

    Begin your Croatian adventure in the historic city of Dubrovnik. After checking into your hotel, visit the enchanting Old Town and walk along the ancient City Walls.

  • Day 2: Dubrovnik city tour and cable car ride to Mount Srđ.

    Join a guided city tour of Dubrovnik and take a cable car ride to Mount Srđ. Enjoy panoramic views of the city and the Adriatic Sea.

  • Day 3: Day trip to the Elafiti Islands.

    Take a day trip to the beautiful Elafiti Islands. Explore the charming islands, swim in crystal-clear waters, and enjoy the serene natural beauty.

  • Day 4: Travel to Split, visit Diocletian's Palace.

    Travel to the vibrant city of Split. Explore the ancient Diocletian's Palace, a UNESCO World Heritage site, and wander through the historic streets.

  • Day 5: Split city tour and Marjan Hill.

    Enjoy a guided city tour of Split and visit Marjan Hill. Discover the city's rich history and enjoy stunning views from the hilltop.

  • Day 6: Day trip to Hvar Island.

    Take a day trip to the picturesque Hvar Island. Explore the charming town of Hvar, visit the historic fortress, and relax on the beautiful beaches.

  • Day 7: Travel to Plitvice Lakes, explore the National Park.

    Head to Plitvice Lakes National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage site. Explore the breathtaking waterfalls, crystal-clear lakes, and lush forests.

  • Day 8: Day at leisure in Plitvice Lakes.

    Spend a relaxing day at leisure in Plitvice Lakes. Enjoy the natural beauty, go for a hike, or simply unwind in the serene surroundings.

  • Day 9: Depart from Zagreb.

    It's time to say goodbye to Croatia. Depart from Zagreb with unforgettable memories of your Croatian adventure.
