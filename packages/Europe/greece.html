Itinerary

  • Day 1: Arrive in Athens, explore the Acropolis and Plaka.

    Begin your Greek odyssey in the historic city of Athens. After checking into your hotel, visit the iconic Acropolis and the charming Plaka district.

  • Day 2: Athens city tour and visit to the National Archaeological Museum.

    Join a guided city tour of Athens and explore the National Archaeological Museum. Discover the city's rich history and cultural treasures.

  • Day 3: Travel to Santorini, enjoy the sunset in Oia.

    Travel to the beautiful island of Santorini. Explore the picturesque villages and enjoy the breathtaking sunset views in Oia.

  • Day 4: Santorini island tour and wine tasting.

    Take an island tour of Santorini and visit local wineries for a wine tasting experience. Enjoy the stunning landscapes and vibrant culture.

  • Day 5: Day at leisure in Santorini.

    Spend a relaxing day at leisure in Santorini. Explore the island at your own pace, visit the beaches, or simply unwind and soak in the beauty.

  • Day 6: Travel to Mykonos, explore Mykonos Town.

    Head to the lively island of Mykonos. Explore Mykonos Town, with its charming streets, vibrant nightlife, and beautiful beaches.

  • Day 7: Delos island tour.

    Take a day trip to the nearby island of Delos, an important archaeological site. Discover the ancient ruins and learn about its fascinating history.

  • Day 8: Day at leisure in Mykonos.

    Spend a day at leisure in Mykonos. Enjoy the island's beaches, try out water sports, or explore the local shops and cafes.

  • Day 9: Depart from Mykonos.

    It's time to say goodbye to Greece. Depart from Mykonos with unforgettable memories of your Greek adventure.
