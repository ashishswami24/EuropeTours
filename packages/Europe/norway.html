Itinerary

  • Day 1: Arrive in Oslo, explore the Royal Palace and Akershus Fortress.

    Begin your Norwegian adventure in the vibrant city of Oslo. After checking into your hotel, visit the Royal Palace and Akershus Fortress.

  • Day 2: Oslo city tour and visit to the Viking Ship Museum.

    Join a guided city tour of Oslo and explore the Viking Ship Museum. Discover the city's rich history and cultural heritage.

  • Day 3: Travel to Bergen, explore Bryggen and Mount Fløyen.

    Travel to the picturesque city of Bergen. Explore the historic Bryggen wharf and take a funicular ride to the top of Mount Fløyen for stunning views.

  • Day 4: Bergen city tour and fjord cruise.

    Enjoy a guided city tour of Bergen, followed by a breathtaking fjord cruise. Experience the stunning landscapes and serene waters.

  • Day 5: Travel to Tromsø, Northern Lights experience.

    Head to Tromsø, known as the Gateway to the Arctic. Enjoy an unforgettable Northern Lights experience and explore the Arctic Cathedral.

  • Day 6: Tromsø city tour and dog sledding adventure.

    Join a guided city tour of Tromsø, followed by an exciting dog sledding adventure. Explore the city's unique Arctic culture and stunning surroundings.

  • Day 7: Day trip to the Lyngen Alps.

    Take a day trip to the Lyngen Alps and enjoy the breathtaking alpine scenery. Experience outdoor activities like hiking, snowshoeing, or simply soaking in the views.

  • Day 8: Day at leisure in Tromsø.

    Spend a relaxing day at leisure in Tromsø. Explore the city at your own pace, visit local attractions, or simply unwind and enjoy the Arctic beauty.

  • Day 9: Return to Oslo, day at leisure.

    Return to Oslo and spend the day at leisure. Explore any remaining attractions, indulge in local cuisine, or simply relax.

  • Day 10: Depart from Oslo.

    It's time to say goodbye to Norway. Depart from Oslo with unforgettable memories of your Norwegian adventure.
