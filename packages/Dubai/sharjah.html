Itinerary

  • Day 1: Arrive in Dubai, explore Burj Khalifa and Dubai Marina.

    Start your journey in Dubai by visiting the iconic Burj Khalifa and Dubai Marina. Enjoy a leisurely evening at the Marina Walk.

  • Day 2: Dubai city tour and dhow cruise.

    Join a guided city tour of Dubai and discover its modern marvels. In the evening, enjoy a traditional dhow cruise with dinner.

  • Day 3: Day at leisure in Dubai.

    Spend a relaxing day at leisure in Dubai. Explore the city at your own pace, visit the beaches, or indulge in some retail therapy.

  • Day 4: Travel to Sharjah, visit Sharjah Museum of Islamic Civilization.

    Travel to Sharjah and visit the fascinating Sharjah Museum of Islamic Civilization. Explore the cultural landmarks and enjoy the beautiful architecture.

  • Day 5: Sharjah city tour and Al Noor Island.

    Enjoy a guided city tour of Sharjah, followed by a visit to Al Noor Island. Experience the blend of nature and art in this serene oasis.

  • Day 6: Depart from Sharjah.

    It's time to say goodbye to the UAE. Depart from Sharjah with unforgettable memories of your journey.
