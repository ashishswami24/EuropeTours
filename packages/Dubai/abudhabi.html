Itinerary

  • Day 1: Arrive in Dubai, explore Burj Khalifa and Dubai Mall.

    Start your journey in Dubai by visiting the iconic Burj Khalifa and Dubai Mall. Enjoy a leisurely evening at the Dubai Fountain.

  • Day 2: Dubai city tour and desert safari.

    Join a guided city tour of Dubai and discover its modern marvels. In the evening, experience an exhilarating desert safari with camel rides and a BBQ dinner.

  • Day 3: Day at leisure in Dubai.

    Spend a relaxing day at leisure in Dubai. Explore the city at your own pace, visit the beaches, or indulge in some retail therapy.

  • Day 4: Travel to Abu Dhabi, visit Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque.

    Travel to Abu Dhabi and visit the stunning Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque. Explore the city's cultural landmarks and enjoy the beautiful architecture.

  • Day 5: Abu Dhabi city tour and Ferrari World.

    Enjoy a guided city tour of Abu Dhabi, followed by a visit to Ferrari World. Experience the thrill of high-speed rides and attractions.

  • Day 6: Day at leisure in Abu Dhabi.

    Spend a day at leisure in Abu Dhabi. Explore the city, visit the beaches, or simply relax and unwind.

  • Day 7: Depart from Abu Dhabi.

    It's time to say goodbye to the UAE. Depart from Abu Dhabi with unforgettable memories of your journey.
