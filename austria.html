Itinerary

  • Day 1: Arrive in Vienna, explore the city and Schonbrunn Palace.

    Start your journey in the beautiful city of Vienna. After checking into your hotel, explore the city and visit the majestic Schonbrunn Palace. Enjoy a leisurely evening in the city.

  • Day 2: Vienna city tour and visit to Belvedere Palace.

    Join a guided city tour of Vienna and visit the famous Belvedere Palace. Enjoy the stunning architecture and beautiful gardens. Spend the afternoon exploring the city's cultural landmarks.

  • Day 3: Travel to Salzburg, enjoy the Sound of Music tour.

    Travel to Salzburg, the birthplace of Mozart. Enjoy a "Sound of Music" tour and visit the iconic locations from the famous movie. Explore the charming streets and historic sites of Salzburg.

  • Day 4: Day trip to Kitzsteinhorn mountain.

    Take a day trip to Kitzsteinhorn mountain and enjoy the breathtaking alpine scenery. Experience thrilling activities like skiing, snowboarding, or simply soak in the stunning views.

  • Day 5: Travel to Innsbruck, visit Swarovski Crystal World.

    Travel to Innsbruck, a picturesque city surrounded by the Alps. Visit Swarovski Crystal World and be amazed by the dazzling crystal exhibits and installations. Spend the evening exploring Innsbruck's charming streets.

  • Day 6: Explore Nordkette Mountain.

    Enjoy a full day exploring Nordkette Mountain. Take a cable car ride to the summit and soak in the panoramic views of the surrounding mountains and valleys. Experience outdoor activities like hiking or simply relax in the scenic surroundings.

  • Day 7: Return to Vienna, day at leisure.

    Return to Vienna and spend the day at leisure. Explore any remaining attractions, indulge in local cuisine, or simply relax and reflect on your journey through Austria.

  • Day 8: Depart from Vienna.

    It's time to say goodbye to Austria. Depart from Vienna with beautiful memories of your Austrian adventure.
